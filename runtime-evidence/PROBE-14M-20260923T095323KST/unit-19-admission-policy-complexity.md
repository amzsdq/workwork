Phase-C promotion criterion remains appropriate: prefer the simplest policy whose long-run utilization and continuation reliability are practically indistinguishable from more complex alternatives. P3/P4 should therefore be promoted only if observed next-unit estimates materially reduce early handoff or overshoot versus P1/P2.

This avoids turning runtime control into a forecasting system unless the measured gain warrants it.
