# R4 coarse-ascent stopping criteria review

## Coarse ascent should stop when

- a credible duration-related failure creates an upper candidate, or
- an external product/runtime ceiling makes further ascent impossible to interpret, or
- risk of losing durable evidence becomes unacceptable before useful boundary information can be gained.

## Coarse ascent should not stop when

- a target merely feels long,
- marginal ideal duty-cycle gain becomes small,
- legacy evidence is sparse,
- one profile has succeeded once.

## Why

The final safety margin and operating cap need information about the upper risk region. Without a failure/ceiling, only a lower bound can be reported.

## R4 implication

A successful 22m + WAKE_OK should lead to 24m coarse ascent rather than immediate cap validation, unless fresh evidence or repository policy changes. A credible 22m duration failure instead triggers W3-controlled refinement.
